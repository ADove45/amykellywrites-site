Ideas, concepts, themes, tropes, genres, plots and character types are not protected by copyright and are often shared by many writers. By submitting, you agree that I may create, edit or publish works that contain ideas or elements similar to those in your submission, whether developed before or after it, and you release me from any claim based on such similarity. This release does not cover your actual written text, which remains yours and will not be copied or published.

  • Timing. I'll read submissions in the order they arrive. Feedback may take a few weeks if many writers take part.
  • Original work only. Please send only writing that is your own. I may decline any submission at my discretion.
  • Age. You must be 18 or older, or have a parent's or guardian's permission to submit.
  • Newsletter. Submitting adds you to my email newsletter. You can unsubscribe anytime.

Questions

Yes. This is for works in progress. A rough first chapter is exactly what Novel November is for.

That's fine. Any novel you're working on this November is welcome, in any genre.

Will my work be used with AI?

No. I read and respond to your pages myself. Your work won't be put into any AI tool, and it will never be used to train AI.

I'm a working author, and ideas, themes and tropes are shared by writers everywhere. I may write or publish work with similar ideas, whether I came up with them before or after reading yours. What I will never do is copy or publish your actual writing. See the idea release in the terms for the details.

I use your pages only to write your feedback, and I delete them once your feedback is sent. You keep all rights to your writing.
