Before you submit

  • It's free. There's no cost and no obligation to hire me for anything afterward.
  • Your work stays yours. You keep all rights to your writing. I won't share, publish or use your work or ideas for anything other than giving you feedback, and I'll delete your files once your feedback is sent.
  • No AI. I read and respond to your pages myself. I won't put your work into any AI tool, and it will never be used to train AI.
  • It's one editor's opinion. My feedback is my honest professional view. It isn't a guarantee of publication, representation or sales.
  • Timing. I'll read submissions in the order they arrive. Feedback may take a few weeks if many writers take part.
  • Original work only. Please send only writing that is your own. I may decline any submission at my discretion.
  • Age. You must be 18 or older, or have a parent's or guardian's permission to submit.
  • Newsletter. Submitting adds you to my email newsletter. You can unsubscribe anytime.
