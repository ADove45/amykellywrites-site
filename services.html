Work with me

Writing & editing services

If you're working on your own manuscript and looking for an editor, ghostwriter or coach, here's how we can work together.

01

Developmental Editing

Big-picture editing for stories that need to work.

I work with authors on structure, pacing, character arcs and narrative voice — the elements that determine whether a manuscript actually works before it goes anywhere near a line edit. My editorial background includes historical fiction, middle grade, YA and genre fiction, including collaborative work on projects like Lifting As We Rise for the Enduring Families Project.

What's included

  • Full-manuscript read-through with an editorial letter (plot, structure, pacing, character, theme)
  • In-line margin notes and a chapter-by-chapter breakdown
  • A follow-up call to walk through revision priorities
  • Optional second pass after revisions

Best for: novelists and memoirists with a complete draft who want a clear, honest roadmap for revision.

02

Ghostwriting & Co-Writing

Your story, your voice — professionally written or polished.

I ghostwrite and co-write fiction across genres, with hands-on experience adapting and producing serialized romance for platforms like ReelShort and DramaWave, as well as original novels under several pen names. Whether you have a fully outlined concept or just a premise and a voice you love, I can help bring it to the page.

What's included

  • Discovery session to define voice, outline and scope
  • Chapter-by-chapter drafting or co-writing on your timeline
  • Revision rounds built into every project
  • Full confidentiality — the work is, and stays, yours

Best for: authors, entrepreneurs or content platforms who need publish-ready fiction without writing every word themselves.

03

Author Coaching & Consulting

Craft guidance and publishing strategy for writers at any stage.

I coach writers through the parts of this business that don't get taught in workshops — from strengthening craft fundamentals to navigating self-publishing and building a sustainable writing practice. This work draws on my own path as a multi-pen-name author, Senior Story Director, and founder of The Hybrid Writer's Workshop.

What's included

  • One-on-one coaching calls (single session or ongoing package)
  • Manuscript or query feedback
  • Self-publishing and KDP guidance
  • Craft mentorship for writers exploring AI-assisted workflows

Best for: emerging or working writers who want a sounding board, accountability or a clearer path forward.
