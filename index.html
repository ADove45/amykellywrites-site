Hi, I'm Amy.

I'm a writer based in Sumter, South Carolina, and I've been publishing my own fiction since 2014. My Gullah Geechee roots shape the stories I tell — the places, the voices, the sense that the past is never far away.

I hold an MFA in Creative Writing from Full Sail University, and my work has ranged from story direction and youth writing education to ghostwriting, developmental editing and technical documentation.

New release

The Daughter Who Borrowed Tomorrow

Episode One: The Tomorrow Market

Time is the only currency that matters. The poor sell it. The rich drink it. And the families who own the Market are always thirsty for more.

Sixteen-year-old Nova Quinn only knows that her mother is dying, the bills are impossible, and the scholarship that was supposed to save them both just went up in smoke. Then her best friend tells her a secret: beneath the city, there's a market where the desperate trade years of their lives for money. Nova is about to discover what she's worth. So is everyone else.
